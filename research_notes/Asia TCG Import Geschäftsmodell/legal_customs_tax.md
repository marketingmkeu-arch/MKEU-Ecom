# Legal, customs and tax framework: importing Asian-language Pokémon TCG products from China and reselling online in DE/EU (as of 27 Oct 2026)

> Method note: The primary-source hosts (zoll.de, taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu, consilium.europa.eu, help.cardmarket.com, lhr-law.de) could **not be fetched** from this research session because of network restrictions. Findings come from web-search snippets of those pages and from secondary sources (IHK, tax advisors, law firms, vendors). Anything marked "verify" should be checked against the primary text before it goes into a checklist. **Changes in 2025/2026 are marked [CHANGED dd.mm.yyyy].**

## 1. Customs: tariff classification, the 2026 low-value reform (€3 duty, €2 handling fee), B2B vs B2C, IOSS, DDP/DAP

### Takeaway
Pokémon cards fall under CN 9504 40 00 (playing cards) at a **2.7%** third-country duty rate. Two sources agree on this, but it was not checked against live TARIC. **[CHANGED 01.07.2026]** The €150 duty exemption ended. Low-value e-commerce consignments now pay a flat **€3 per tariff category (not per piece)** until 1 July 2028. **[PLANNED 01.11.2026]** A separate EU handling fee of **€2** is due, charged to traders or platforms. Whether it counts per item, per category/line or per parcel is still disputed in the sources. For a German reseller, a bulk B2B import (commercial invoice, regular customs declaration, 2.7% duty plus 19% EUSt) is the normal route. IOSS exists only for B2C distance sales of consignments ≤ €150 shipped directly from outside the EU to consumers.

### Cited Findings
**Tariff classification / duty rate**
- 9504.40.00.00.0 "Spielkarten" applies to card games made of paper or board. Third-country duty is **2.7%**, and the page specific to Pokémon cards also gives 9504.40.00.00.0 at 2.7% — [zolltarifnummer.com: Pokémon-Karte](https://www.zolltarifnummer.com/einreihung/pokemon-karte); [zolltarifnummer.com: Spielkarte](https://www.zolltarifnummer.com/einreihung/spielkarte)
- A second database lists EU 95044000 at a third-country duty of **2.700% erga omnes**, with 0% preference only for Bosnia, North Macedonia and Kosovo. **China has no preference** — [zolltarifnummern.de 95044000](https://www.zolltarifnummern.de/2025/95044000)
- Games that include significant extra components such as a board or figures fall under 9504.90.80 instead. This matters for mixed products like trainer boxes with dice, markers or playmats: verify per product — [zolltarifnummer.com: Brettspiel](https://www.zolltarifnummer.com/einreihung/brettspiel)
- Caution: one aggregator classifies "Sammelkarte" generically under 4911.99.00 (printed matter). For game cards, Pokémon-specific entries and US CBP rulings (e.g. N359352 "Pokémon trading cards from Japan") use 9504.40 — [zolltarifnummer.com: Sammelkarte](https://www.zolltarifnummer.com/einreihung/sammelkarte); [CBP ruling N359352 via tarifflens](https://www.tarifflens.ai/rulings/N359352)
- Snippets showed no live EU TARIC lookup. Verify the rate and any measures (e.g. anti-dumping, which is unlikely for 9504) in TARIC or with a binding tariff information (vZTA) before the first import.

**End of the €150 exemption / €3 flat duty [CHANGED 01.07.2026]**
- European Commission: "From 1 July 2026, the EU will apply a temporary €3 customs duty per item on low-value consignments (up to €150) imported from outside the EU, abolishing the duty exemption applicable until 30 June 2026 … flat fee, which will apply until 1 July 2028, after which normal customs duties will apply" — [EC news 29.06.2026](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/ensuring-fairness-and-safety-eur3-customs-duty-low-value-parcels-2026-06-29_en); [TAXUD guidance & legal text 08.06.2026](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/guidance-and-legal-text-temporary-flat-fee-low-value-imports-which-will-apply-until-1-july-2028-2026-06-08_en)
- Council (12.12.2025): the €3 is applied to "each different item, according to their tariff headings, contained in a consignment". The Council refers to non-EU sellers registered in IOSS, who account for 93% of e-commerce flows — [Consilium press release 12.12.2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/)
- zoll.de: "Pauschalzoll von 3 Euro je Warenkategorie". In its example, socks, cable ties and trousers in one parcel cost €9 — [zoll.de Internetbestellungen](https://www.zoll.de/DE/Privatpersonen/Postsendungen-Internetbestellungen/Sendungen-aus-einem-Nicht-EU-Staat/Zoll-und-Steuern/Internetbestellungen/internetbestellungen_node.html); [zoll.de Wegfall der 150-Euro-Zollfreigrenze](https://www.zoll.de/DE/Fachthemen/Zoelle/Wegfall-der-150-Euro-Zollfreigrenze/wegfall-der-150-euro-zollfreigrenze_node.html)
- Legal basis per IHK Pfalz: **Regulation (EU) 2026/382** and **Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1200**. The flat duty applies regardless of which VAT procedure is used — [IHK Pfalz](https://www.ihk.de/pfalz/international/export-import/handel-mit-drittlaendern4/import/einfuehrung-von-importzoll-auf-kleinsendungen-6936500)
- Collection: per IHK Nordschwarzwald, parcel carriers collect it from the foreign sender or principal, not from the recipient. Other guides say the carrier pays customs and invoices the party responsible — [IHK Nordschwarzwald](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/international/export/import/einfuhrverfahren/abschaffung-der-150-euro-freigrenze-6828486); [Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/blog/eu-end-150-customs-duty-exemption-2026.html)
- **Conflict on scope:** some guides say the €3 applies to IOSS/postal distance sales only ([count.tax](https://count.tax/wissen/ioss); [ecom-tools.de](https://ecom-tools.de/zollfreigrenze-abgeschafft/)). Others, citing the TAXUD guidance, say it is not limited to IOSS/H7 and applies with or without IOSS ([fillhub.de](https://fillhub.de/zollfreigrenze-abgeschafft/); [PR-Web 15.10.2026](https://www.prweb.de/2026/10/15/pauschalzoll-von-3-euro-trifft-geschenkbestellungen-aus-drittstaaten-der-zoll-fertigte-2025-knapp-790-millionen-warenpositionen-ab/)). Consignments above €150 keep the regular tariff rate — [fillhub.de](https://fillhub.de/zollfreigrenze-abgeschafft/)
- The flat duty is to be replaced by exact value-based duty once the EU customs data hub for e-commerce is operational — [IHK Nordschwarzwald](https://www.ihk.de/nordschwarzwald/international/export/import/einfuhrverfahren/abschaffung-der-150-euro-freigrenze-6828486)
- Card-specific explainer on the €3 duty for Pokémon cards — [cardsense.pro](https://www.cardsense.pro/guides/eu-customs-3-euro-duty-pokemon-cards-july-2026)

**€2 handling fee [PLANNED/CHANGED 01.11.2026]**
- IHK Chemnitz: "Kleinsendungen: 2 Euro Bearbeitungsgebühr ab November" — [IHK Chemnitz](https://www.ihk.de/chemnitz/international/zoll-und-bescheinigungsdienst/laenderinformationen/import-2-euro-bearbeitungsgebuehr-7203034)
- The fee is a handling charge, not a customs duty. Per the draft it applies regardless of value, including goods above €150. **Platforms and traders are liable, not consumers**, and costs passed on to buyers must respect price-labelling rules (PAngV). Before application, the legal act still had to be published in the OJ, with application 10 days after entry into force — [LHR law firm](https://www.lhr-law.de/magazin/wettbewerbsrecht-kartellrecht/eu-bearbeitungsgebuehr-2-euro-onlinehandel/); [etailment](https://www.etailment.de/magazin/2-euro-pro-artikel-ab-november-was-die-eu-zollreform-fuer-shops-und-plattformen-aendert)
- **Conflict on the counting unit:** zoll.de says per goods category. IHK Regensburg and wortfilter say per line (Warenposition) in the customs declaration, not per parcel. IHK Rhein-Neckar and DHL say per parcel — [IHK Regensburg](https://www.ihk.de/regensburg/fachthemen/international/nachrichten-international/eu-bearbeitungsgebuehr-fuer-kleinsendungen-7187844); [wortfilter](https://wortfilter.de/eu-zollreform-bearbeitungsgebuehr-2-euro-warenposition/); [IHK Rhein-Neckar](https://www.ihk.de/rhein-neckar/international/export-import/einfuhr/zoelle-fuer-kleinsendungen-aus-drittstaaten-seit-1-juli-2026--4882662); [DHL](https://www.dhl.com/gb-en/ecommerce/uk/international-parcel-delivery/eu-customs-changes-2026.html)
- One article cites the legal basis as Art. 20 of Regulation (EU) 2026/2108 (new Union Customs Code), "EUR 2 per item". **Unverified** — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/18538/eu-handling-fee-2-euro-non-eu-parcels)
- Member states may add national surcharges: France €2 since 01.03.2026, Italy from 2026 (start dates reported differently). Germany: none found — [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/eu/eu-e2-customs-fee-states-go-early/); [Cullen International](https://www.cullen-international.com/news/2026/05/Some-EU-member-states-pre-empt-EU-level-small-parcel-handling-fee.html); [douana.ch](https://douana.ch/eu-gebuehren-kleinsendungen-2026-ueberblick/)

**IOSS**
- IOSS covers distance sales of imported goods in consignments with an intrinsic value ≤ €150, B2C only (non-taxable buyers), no excise goods. It is optional, needs one registration for all 27 member states, and is filed monthly — [Wikipedia IOSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Import_One-Stop_Shop); [EAS Project](https://help.easproject.com/hc/en-gb/articles/5022149438879-What-is-IOSS)
- EU-established sellers can register for IOSS directly in their home member state without an intermediary. Non-EU sellers from countries without a mutual assistance agreement, such as China, need an EU intermediary — [Stripe on IOSS for German companies](https://stripe.com/en-cn/resources/more/import-one-stop-shop-ioss); [eclear](https://eclear.com/topic/import-one-stop-shop/)

### Inferences
- **Bulk B2B import (recommended baseline):** a consolidated shipment (air or sea freight, or courier with formal entry) to the reseller's own address in DE, with the reseller as importer (EORI number required), pays 2.7% duty on the customs value (CIF) plus 19% EUSt. Once stock is in DE, the onward sales are domestic or intra-EU sales, so IOSS, the €3 flat duty and probably the €2 handling fee do not apply to them (verify for the €2 fee if the shipment value is ≤ €150). The €3 regime only targets ≤ €150 consignments, so a consignment above €150 is cleared at 2.7%.
- **Example (illustrative, not sourced; check the EUSt basis in §11 UStG):** goods €1,000 plus freight/insurance to the EU border €100 = customs value €1,100. Duty at 2.7% = €29.70. EUSt at 19% on (€1,100 + €29.70 + any onward transport costs to the destination in DE) ≈ €214.64. Total import levies ≈ €244, or about 22% on the goods value. For a Kleinunternehmer, the €214.64 EUSt is a **real cost**; for a VAT-registered seller it is recoverable input VAT.
- **Small test orders (≤ €150) via courier from China:** €3 per tariff category, roughly €3 for a parcel of only cards, versus 2.7% of €150 = €4.05. Add the €2 handling fee from Nov 2026 if it applies, plus 19% EUSt and courier clearance fees (often €5–15+ for DHL/UPS/FedEx; not sourced here). Small parcels therefore cost proportionally more.
- **Dropshipping from China directly to EU consumers:** the German seller would be the supplier in a distance sale of imported goods. Using IOSS, the seller charges the destination-country VAT at checkout, and the €3 duty and €2 fee become trader costs. **DDP** (seller pays duty and VAT up front, e.g. via IOSS or a DDP courier) avoids surprise charges for the consumer. **DAP** makes the consumer pay EUSt, duty and carrier fees on delivery, which tends to cause refusals, returns and bad reviews, and under the platform rules the seller still remains the trader. This model is weak for a Kleinunternehmer (see Section 2) and also conflicts with GPSR and eBay rules (see Sections 4 and 6).

### Gaps
- No live TARIC confirmation of the 2.7% rate (TARIC and zoll.de could not be fetched).
- The final adopted legal text for the €2 handling fee (OJ reference, counting unit, application to B2B imports and to EU-warehouse parcels) is unconfirmed. Treat €2 per category/line as the planning assumption and verify on zoll.de in Nov 2026.
- Whether the €3 duty applies to non-IOSS commercial consignments ≤ €150 imported by a German business for resale (B2B, H7 vs H1 declaration) is unresolved because the sources conflict. Ask a customs broker or the Zoll-Infocenter.

## 2. VAT: import VAT, Kleinunternehmerregelung after the 2025 reform, OSS, IOSS, marketplaces

### Takeaway
**[CHANGED 01.01.2025, JStG 2024]** §19 UStG thresholds are now **€25,000 net prior year / €100,000 net current year**. The €100k limit is a hard cap: Kleinunternehmer status ends the moment it is exceeded. In the founding year the cap is €25,000. A Kleinunternehmer **cannot deduct input VAT, including the 19% EUSt**. That makes EUSt a direct cost of about 19% on every import, which hurts a goods-import model with low margins. Opting out (Verzicht) is binding for 5 years. Intra-EU B2C distance sales above €10,000 per year (EU-wide) are taxed in the destination country via OSS.

### Cited Findings
- Thresholds: prior year ≤ €25,000 and current year ≤ €100,000, both net. Set by JStG 2024 effective 1 Jan 2025. BMF letter (Erlass) of **18.03.2025** — [IHK Stuttgart](https://www.ihk.de/stuttgart/fuer-unternehmen/recht-und-steuern/steuerrecht/umsatzsteuer-national/kleinunternehmerregelung-in-der-umsatzsteuer-1843632); [IHK Nordwestfalen](https://www.ihk.de/nordwestfalen/steuern/kleinunternehmerregleung-2025-6412924)
- Actual turnover counts, not a forecast. If turnover exceeds €100,000, the status ends from that moment, with the sale that crosses the line. In the founding year the limit is €25,000, and regular taxation applies from the sale that exceeds it — [IHK Nürnberg Merkblatt 116-25](https://www.ihk-nuernberg.de/fileadmin/IHK_Nuernberg/Recht_Steuern/Merkblaetter/Kleinunternehmer_116-25.pdf); [selbststaendigkeit.de](https://selbststaendigkeit.de/buchhaltung-fuer-gruender/umsatzsteuer-bei-kleinunternehmen/)
- No input VAT deduction: "weder bei Eingangsleistungen im Inland noch bei Einfuhrumsatzsteuer oder Reverse-Charge-Leistungen zum Vorsteuerabzug berechtigt"; "Ebensowenig kann er die Einfuhrumsatzsteuer als Vorsteuer abziehen" — [IHK Nürnberg Merkblatt](https://www.ihk-nuernberg.de/fileadmin/IHK_Nuernberg/Recht_Steuern/Merkblaetter/Kleinunternehmer_116-25.pdf); [IHK Ostwürttemberg](https://www.ihk.de/ostwuerttemberg/produktmarken/recht/steuerrecht-neu/kleinunternehmerregelung-in-der-umsatzsteuer-4857380)
- Opting out of §19 binds for at least 5 calendar years. For 2025 the waiver can be declared until 28 Feb 2027 (the deadline for the annual return) — [IHK (search snippet, several IHK pages)](https://www.ihk.de/koeln/hauptnavigation/recht-steuern/steuern/kleinunternehmer-im-umsatzsterrecht-5695056)
- Finanzamt BW explainer on the Kleinunternehmerregelung — [Finanzamt BW](https://finanzamt-bw.fv-bwl.de/%2CLde/Startseite/Service/Was%2Bist%2Bdie%2Bsogenannte%2BKleinunternehmerregelung_)
- **OSS:** the €10,000 net threshold is EU-wide (the sum of all cross-border B2C sales of goods and e-services), not per country. Once it is exceeded in the current or prior year, destination-country VAT applies from the point it was crossed. The seller can waive the threshold voluntarily (§3c Abs. 4 S. 2 UStG). OSS returns are quarterly, and the rules are unchanged in 2026 (ViDA platform changes expected from 2028) — [kostenlose-erechnung.de OSS 2026](https://kostenlose-erechnung.de/ratgeber/oss-verfahren-eu-onestopshop/); [pandotax](https://pandotax.de/rechtliches/oss-regelung/); [IHK Ulm](https://www.ihk.de/ulm/hauptnavigation/mitgliedsunternehmen/beraten-informieren/recht-steuern/auskuenfte-zum-steuerrecht/umsatzsteuer-verbrauchssteuer/umsatzsteuer-international/umsetzung-zweite-stufe-mehrwertsteuerdigitalpaket-6711014)
- Kleinunternehmer and OSS: OSS is effectively irrelevant to a Kleinunternehmer below the €10k threshold. Above it, destination VAT applies, which requires charging VAT — [pandotax](https://pandotax.de/ecommerce/lieferschwellen-umsatzsteuerpflicht/); [lexware](https://www.lexware.de/wissen/buchhaltung-finanzen/one-stop-shop/)
- IOSS is B2C ≤ €150 consignments only (see Section 1) — [EAS](https://help.easproject.com/what-is-ioss)
- Cardmarket reports sellers above 30 transactions or €2,000 per calendar year to tax authorities annually (DAC7 / PStTG) — [Cardmarket Tax Requirements](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/TaxRequirements)

### Inferences
- **When regular taxation pays off (rough logic, needs a tax advisor):** for a goods importer selling mostly to consumers, the Kleinunternehmer advantage is that no VAT is added to the sale price. The cost is non-deductible input VAT (19% EUSt on goods plus duty plus freight, and VAT on platform fees, packaging and so on). If prices are market-driven (Cardmarket/eBay set the price regardless of the seller's VAT status), a regularly taxed seller remits 19/119 ≈ 16% of gross sales but recovers 19% of costs. Kleinunternehmer status is only better when gross margin over landed cost is high. Rough break-even: KU is better when VAT on sales (0.16 × gross sales) exceeds the recoverable input VAT (0.19 × net purchases including EUSt base). That is, KU wins roughly when purchase costs are below about 84% of gross sales, which is usually the case for a profitable reseller. **Caution:** this is a simplification. B2B buyers (shops) prefer invoices with VAT, and the 5-year lock-in matters. Have a Steuerberater model it.
- **Practical consequence:** above €100k turnover in the current year, KU status ends mid-year, and invoices must switch to VAT immediately. Prices on marketplaces must be adjusted.
- **Kleinunternehmer and EU-wide sales:** KU status under §19 covers German turnover. B2C sales to other EU countries above the €10k EU threshold are taxable in the destination country, so the seller would need OSS registration and must charge foreign VAT even as a German KU. There is also the separate EU-KU scheme (§19a UStG, from 2025) for using small-business exemptions in other member states, but it is not researched here. **Flag for tax advisor.**
- **Marketplaces as deemed supplier (§3 Abs. 3a UStG / Art. 14a VAT Directive):** applies only where a marketplace facilitates (a) distance sales of imported goods ≤ €150 or (b) sales by non-EU sellers of goods located in the EU. A German-established seller selling stock held in DE is **not** covered. The seller remains the supplier and VAT debtor. (General knowledge; no fetched source; verify.)

### Gaps
- No primary source fetched on §19a UStG (EU-wide small-business scheme) or its interaction with OSS.
- No BMF source on whether a Kleinunternehmer may or must register for OSS once over the €10k threshold. Steuerberater needed.

## 3. Trademark: parallel import of Asian-language cards (exhaustion, enforcement)

### Takeaway
Trademark exhaustion in the EU is **EEA-wide only** (Art. 15 EUTMR / §24 MarkenG). Pokémon products first sold by the rights holder in China, Japan, Korea or elsewhere outside the EEA are **not exhausted**. The rights holder (The Pokémon Company / Nintendo / Creatures / Game Freak, depending on the mark) can therefore prohibit their import and sale in the EU without consent, even when the goods are genuine. The burden of proving exhaustion is on the seller. No documented EU court case or Cardmarket takedown campaign against Asian-language Pokémon cards was found. Anecdotal eBay VeRO removals exist.

### Cited Findings
- EU/German IP law has no worldwide exhaustion, only Union/EEA-wide exhaustion. Placing goods on the market outside the EEA does not exhaust the right, and the rights holder can prohibit import without consent — [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/markenrecht/13344-erschoepfung-des-markenrechts.html); [Bardehle Pagenberg: Parallelimport und Außenseitervertrieb (PDF)](https://www.bardehle.com/de/ip-news-wissen/publikation-/publikationen/pdf/parallelimport-und-aussenseitervertrieb.pdf)
- OLG Nürnberg, 29.10.2024 – 3 U 881/24: under the clear wording of the law, exhaustion under §24 Abs. 1 MarkenG cannot arise from placing goods on the market outside the EEA, even in Turkey despite the association agreement — [gesetze-bayern.de](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Document/Y-300-Z-GRURRS-B-2024-N-30069?hl=true)
- **[2025]** BGH reportedly confirmed this in July 2025, case I ZR 226/24 (secondary source; judgment not read) — [Ferner Alsdorf](https://www.ferner-alsdorf.de/keine-erschoepfung-von-unionsmarkenrechten-bei-inverkehrbringen-in-der-tuerkei/)
- Burden of proof: whoever relies on §24 I MarkenG must prove exhaustion (BGH line of case law) — [BGH 12.12.2002 I ZR 133/00 via rewis](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/96a-11-12-2002-i-zr-13300/); [ferner-alsdorf Parallelimport](https://www.ferner-alsdorf.de/thema/parallelimport/)
- eBay community thread: a seller's Pokémon listings were removed via VeRO after notices by The Pokémon Company International through Corsearch. The seller suspected the parallel-import status was the reason (anecdotal, US-centric) — [eBay community](https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/Anyone-got-their-Pok%C3%A9mon-Listings-removed-Due-to-VeRO-and/td-p/34936918)
- **[2026]** TPCi had eBay remove early 30th-anniversary listings under the stolen-property policy, because the goods had not been distributed through authorized channels by that date — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-cracks-down-on-early-30th-anniversary-card-sales-over-stolen-property-concerns-3402079/); [Resell Calendar](https://resellcalendar.com/news/news/pokemon-tcg-reseller-rules-banned-30th-celebration/)
- Japanese-language cards are openly listed on eBay.de (example listing) — [eBay.de listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/388754783879)

### Inferences
- Legally, selling Chinese, Japanese or Korean sealed Pokémon products in the EU **without rights-holder consent is a trademark infringement risk** (cease-and-desist with injunction, damages, destruction claims, customs seizure under Reg. (EU) 608/2013 if the rights holder has filed a customs application). Whether The Pokémon Company actually enforces this against Asian-language products in the EU is not documented in the sources found. The market (Cardmarket, eBay.de) tolerates these products widely today, but tolerance is not consent.
- Singles first sold in the EEA (resale of cards already on the EEA market) are exhausted. The risk concentrates on the **first import** of sealed product from Asia.
- Counterfeit risk with Chinese suppliers (fake Pokémon products are common) would be a separate and much more serious infringement, including possible customs seizure and criminal liability under §143 MarkenG. **Lawyer (Markenrecht) consultation strongly recommended** before scaling.

### Gaps
- No EU/DE court decision or documented takedown specifically about Asian-language Pokémon TCG products was found.
- Whether The Pokémon Company has filed an EU customs application (AFA) under Reg. 608/2013 was not found. A lawyer could check this.
- Cardmarket's policy on Asian-language products (it lists Japanese, Korean and Chinese cards in some games) could not be fetched.

## 4. Product compliance: GPSR, toy safety, VerpackG/LUCID

### Takeaway
**[Since 13.12.2024]** The GPSR (EU) 2023/988 applies. Products from China need an **EU-based responsible economic operator** (Art. 16). When the German reseller imports in its own name, it is the **importer** and must put its own name and address on the product or packaging, alongside the manufacturer details, plus a type or batch number. Online listings must show manufacturer details, the responsible person, product identifiers and warnings (Art. 19). Trading card games are generally treated as toys (Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC: CE mark, importer obligations). The new Toy Safety Regulation (EU) 2025/2509 was published on 12.12.2025 and mostly applies later (sources say 2030, conflicting). VerpackG: register in LUCID **and** join a dual system from the first shipping package. Fines reach €100k/€200k. ElektroG does not apply.

### Cited Findings
- GPSR: a product cannot be placed on the market without an EU-based economic operator. Direct importers from China or dropshippers need a named EU responsible person per product: the EU manufacturer, the importer or a written-mandate authorized representative — [shopio24](https://shopio24.de/wissen/gpsr-produktsicherheit-shopify-shop); [chinasend](https://chinasend.de/wissen-gpsr-china-import); [dropshipping-forum](https://dropshipping-forum.de/eu-verordnung-ab-dem-13-dezember-2024-gehoert-der-hersteller-auf-deine-produktseite/)
- Labelling: name and contact address of the manufacturer and, where applicable, the importer, plus a type, batch or serial number on the product or packaging. Distributors must check that these are present (Art. 12) — [Händlerbund GPSR](https://www.haendlerbund.de/de/ratgeber/recht/produktsicherheitsverordnung); [J&J Ideenschmiede](https://jj-ideenschmiede.de/blog/die-neue-gpsr-richtlinie-im-e-commerce-was-sie-wissen-muessen/)
- Marketplaces block or deactivate listings without EU responsible-person data (Amazon, Kaufland examples) — [sellercore](https://sellercore.io/amazon-gpsr-produktsicherheitsverordnung-verantwortliche-person); [amzplus Kaufland 2026](https://amzplus-consulting.de/blog/kaufland-eu-verantwortlicher-bevollmaechtigter-2026)
- Card games sold in Europe must comply with the Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC (or the GPSD/GPSR). Products for play by children aged 0–14 are toys and need a CE mark — [compliancegate](https://www.compliancegate.com/how-to-ce-mark-toys/)
- Cartamundi (card manufacturer) guide: standard playing-card games need no age warning, but small parts need Annex V warnings — [Cartamundi legal markings PDF](https://cartamundi.com/app/uploads/2019/08/Legal-markings-and-warnings-on-toys_v4-english.pdf)
- **[NEW 12.12.2025]** Toy Safety Regulation (EU) 2025/2509 was published in the OJ on 12.12.2025 and entered into force on 1 Jan 2026. Majority view: it applies from **1 Aug 2030**, with transitional provisions. It introduces a digital product passport. Outliers give 2026 dates — [Bureau Veritas](https://www.cps.bureauveritas.com/newsroom/eu-toy-safety-regulation-20252509-published); [Eurofins](https://www.eurofins.com/en/consumer-product-testing/softlines-hardlines/news-articles/a-quick-overview-of-the-new-eu-toy-safety-regulation-eu-20252509/); [Intertek](https://www.intertek.com/products-retail/insight-bulletins/2025/1504-eu-published-the-regulation-on-the-safety-of-toys/); contra [Chemradar](https://www.chemradar.com/en/service/detail/f7u5uqfplx4w)
- VerpackG: LUCID registration **and** dual-system participation are both mandatory from the first package to consumers. Since July 2022, marketplaces must check registration. Fines up to €100,000 (registration) and €200,000 (system participation). Registration cannot be delegated — [ecom-tools LUCID](https://ecom-tools.de/lucid-verpackungsregister/); [packa](https://contenthub.packa.com/lucid-verpackungsregister-pflichten-fristen-fehler); [channelpartner](https://www.channelpartner.de/article/3895441/was-haendler-jetzt-wissen-muessen.html)
- **[Possible change 12.08.2026, unverified]** One source says the EU Packaging Regulation (PPWR) plus a new German implementing act replace the VerpackG from 12 Aug 2026, while the registration, system and reporting duties remain — [deutsche-recycling.de](https://deutsche-recycling.de/blog/verpackungsgesetz-lucid/)

### Inferences
- If the Chinese-market products (e.g. Simplified Chinese Pokémon TCG by its Chinese licensee) carry **no CE mark and no EU importer data**, the German importer takes on the importer obligations under the toy directive: check CE marking, the declaration of conformity and technical documentation from the manufacturer, add its own name and address, and provide German warnings. For Asian-market sealed products this is likely **impossible to obtain from the rights holder**. That is a real compliance gap, combined with the trademark issue. The packaging of Japanese Pokémon products is not designed for the EU market. **Lawyer/compliance consultant needed.**
- Whether TCG cards count as "toys" or "collectibles for adults" is arguable. The Pokémon TCG is marketed for ages 6+, which points to toy status. No authoritative source specific to Pokémon was found.
- ElektroG is not relevant (no electrical components).

### Gaps
- eBay's and Cardmarket's specific GPSR listing fields could not be fetched. eBay.de requires manufacturer and responsible-person fields since Dec 2024, from general knowledge; verify on the eBay help pages.
- No source on how market surveillance treats Asian-language TCG products without CE marking.

## 5. Consumer law for commercial online sellers, plus eBay and Cardmarket seller status

### Takeaway
Commercial sellers owe consumers a 14-day withdrawal right (§312g, §355 BGB) with a proper Widerrufsbelehrung, an Impressum (§5 DDG, which replaced §5 TMG on 14.05.2024), a 2-year statutory warranty (Gewährleistung, §437 BGB) and correct price indications (PAngV). **Sealed booster packs or boxes do not fall under any §312g Abs. 2 exception:** sealing is not a hygiene or data-carrier seal, and cards are not inseparably mixed. The seller can only claim compensation for loss of value (Wertersatz) for opened packs. Cardmarket requires "Professional" status (trade licence plus ID) for commercial sellers. The fee level could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- §312g Abs. 1 BGB gives a withdrawal right for distance contracts. The exceptions in Abs. 2 are exhaustive — [gesetze.legal §312g](https://gesetze.legal/bund/bgb/312g); [dejure §312g](https://dejure.org/gesetze/BGB_ab_01.01.2023/312g.html)
- IT-Recht Kanzlei: there is no statutory exclusion for opened TCG boosters with random contents. The unsealing exception only covers hygiene goods and data carriers. The mixing exception (§312g Abs. 2 Nr. 4) needs inseparability, which individually identifiable cards are not. The remedy is Wertersatz, i.e. the difference between the pack price and the market value of the returned cards (law-firm opinion, no court ruling) — [IT-Recht Kanzlei: Widerruf Ausschluss Sammelkarten Booster](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/widerruf-ausschluss-sammelkarten-booster.html); [IT-Recht Kanzlei: Wertersatz](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/Kommentar/20285/Wertersatz_nicht_Erstattungswert.php); [IT-Recht Kanzlei Fallsammlung](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/ausschluss-widerrufsrecht-fallsammlung.html)
- Händlerbund offers legal-text packages (AGB, Widerruf, Impressum) for Cardmarket sellers from €9.90/month — [Händlerbund Cardmarket](https://www.haendlerbund.de/de/verkaufsplattformen/cardmarket)
- Cardmarket seller categories are Private, Professional and Power. Private-seller sales are restricted when volume suggests commercial trading. Professional sellers need ID, phone number and a business registration or trade licence, and have no selling limits. Between professionals, an invoice must be issued on request. Professionals can apply for pre-sale status — [Cardmarket Professional Seller Guide](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/professional-seller-guide); [Cardmarket New User Guide](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/new-user-guide); [Cardmarket PreSales](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/PreSales)
- Cardmarket's DAC7 reporting threshold is 30 transactions or €2,000 per year — [Cardmarket Tax Requirements](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/TaxRequirements)
- A third-party claim that private accounts are flagged at about €2,000 per year in sales is unverified — [cardpulse.club guide 2026](https://www.cardpulse.club/blog/cardmarket-complete-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Commercial sellers on Cardmarket and eBay need: Impressum, AGB, Widerrufsbelehrung plus a model withdrawal form, Datenschutzerklärung, a statutory warranty notice, gross prices with VAT and shipping (or a §19 note for a KU), GPSR product information, and LUCID. As a commercial seller you cannot exclude warranty for new goods, and you cannot exclude withdrawal for sealed product.
- Pre-sales of Asian sets are common. Distance-selling rules on delivery-date information (Art. 246a EGBGB) apply.

### Gaps
- **Cardmarket commission:** the commonly cited figure of 5% (plus payment fees, around 1%) for all sellers could **not be verified**. The fee page was not reachable. Check help.cardmarket.com "Fees" directly.
- eBay.de commercial-seller fees and obligations were not fetched. eBay.de requires commercial sellers to register as gewerblich and provide legal texts (general knowledge).

## 6. eBay drop-shipping policy

### Takeaway
eBay allows drop shipping only from a wholesale supplier. It **prohibits** listing an item and then buying it from another retailer or marketplace (Amazon, AliExpress and so on) to ship directly to the buyer. This is sometimes called "retail arbitrage", though the term is used inconsistently. The exact official German wording could not be retrieved, only the help-page URL.

### Cited Findings
- Official eBay.de help page "Direktversand" (drop shipping): describes it as a selling option where the seller does not handle processing and shipping themselves — [eBay.de Hilfe: Direktversand (id=4176)](https://www.ebay.de/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/drop-shipping?id=4176)
- Policy content as reported: allowed only via wholesale suppliers or manufacturers with an agreement. Prohibited: "listing an item and then purchasing the item from another retailer or marketplace that ships directly to your customer". Penalties include listing removal, selling limits, loss of Top Rated status and suspension — [SaleHoo 2026](https://www.salehoo.com/learn/ebay-dropshipping); [eseller365](https://eseller365.com/ebays-updated-drop-shipping-policy-bans-arbitrage-creates-other-policy-conflict/); [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/?p=29996)
- Fulfilling eBay orders from Amazon, Walmart, AliExpress or any other retailer or marketplace violates the policy — [cahoot.ai](https://www.cahoot.ai/ebay-dropshipping/); [eprolo](https://eprolo.com/de/dropshipping-on-ebay)
- Terminology conflict: eseller365 notes that classic retail arbitrage (buying discounted goods and stocking them) is **not** prohibited. Only buying after the sale from a retailer is prohibited — [eseller365](https://eseller365.com/ebays-updated-drop-shipping-policy-bans-arbitrage-creates-other-policy-conflict/)

### Inferences
- Holding stock in Germany (bulk import) complies with the policy. Dropshipping from a Chinese wholesaler with an agreement is allowed in principle, but it is impractical given customs, GPSR, delivery times and trademark issues. Fulfilling from retail platforms such as Taobao or Pinduoduo after the sale is prohibited.

### Gaps
- Verbatim official policy wording (ebay.de / ebay.com) not retrieved. The page is blocked for automated access, so verify manually at the URL above.
