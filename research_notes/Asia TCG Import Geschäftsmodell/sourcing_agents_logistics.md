## Q1: Which agents and forwarders are reputable in 2026, what do they charge, and what does shipping to DE cost for about 5–20 kg?

### Takeaway
Superbuy, CSSBuy and Sugargoo are the most established China purchasing agents still running. Pandabuy is gone. Hoobuy and ACBuy grew mainly by serving the counterfeit "rep" scene, which is a reputational and compliance risk for a legitimate reseller. Service fees run from 0% to about 12%, and FX markups are a hidden cost. Several agents sell "tax-included / IOSS" DHL or DPD lines to Germany for parcels up to 10–20 kg, with transit of about 10–20 days. I found no verified €/kg price for a 5–20 kg parcel to DE; it has to be quoted in the agents' calculators. For Japan, Buyee and ZenMarket charge about ¥300 per item (Mar 2026), with ZenMarket's fee structure disputed.

### Inferences
- For a business, the "tax-included / IOSS" agent lines are built for B2C consumer imports, where the agent or carrier acts as IOSS intermediary. A German reseller importing stock to resell should import in its own name (own EORI, import VAT deductible as Vorsteuer). A consumer DDP line may not give a proper import VAT document (Einfuhrumsatzsteuerbescheid) that allows input-VAT deduction. Clarify with the agent and a Steuerberater.
- Agents are best suited to small top-ups (5–20 kg). Above roughly 20–30 kg per month, a consolidated forwarder shipment in the company's own name is probably cheaper and cleaner (see Q3/Q5).
- Agent FX markups (3–11%) can outweigh a nominal "0% service fee". Compare the total landed cost, not the headline fee.

- No primary fee data for Basetao, Hoobuy or FromJapan.
- No published insurance rates or compensation caps were found for any agent.

## Q3: How do agents handle EU customs after the €150 duty exemption ended on 1 July 2026, and what do freight forwarders charge?

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2026 (Council Regulation (EU) 2026/382), low-value consignments of €150 or less are no longer duty-free. A temporary flat duty of €3 per item type (per HS-code line) applies to IOSS e-commerce consignments until July 2028. **B2B imports to VAT-registered businesses pay standard duty instead of the €3 flat rate.** Import VAT (19% in DE) applies either way. Agent "tax-free" lines are B2C DDP products. There is a forum claim that Superbuy lets users declare any value. **Under-declaring is customs and tax fraud and must not be used.** For stock imports, use your own EORI with a DAP or DDP forwarder. Indicative Sep 2026 rates: air about $4.3–4.9/kg (for 1,000 kg and up), LCL sea about $45–120/CBM plus fixed charges.

### Cited Findings
**EU rules**
- The €150 duty exemption was removed on 1 July 2026. The €3 flat duty runs until 1 July 2028, mainly for IOSS goods. — [Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/us/en/blog/2025/11/eu-end-150-customs-duty-exemption-2026); [Andersen BE](https://be.andersen.com/en/news/the-end-of-the-eur-150-customs-duty-exemption-the-european-union-introduces-a-flat-rate-eur-3-duty-on-small-parcels); [Council press release, 12 Dec 2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/12/customs-council-agrees-to-levy-customs-duty-on-small-parcels-as-of-1-july-2026/); [LexisNexis 01/07/2026](https://www.lexisnexis.co.uk/legal/news/commission-abolishes-150-customs-duty-exemption-for-low-value-e-commerce-imports-introduces-3)
- The €3 applies per item type, meaning per HS tariff line. A parcel containing several HS codes triggers several €3 charges. The carrier or broker pays the duty and invoices the IOSS holder. The duty is not part of the IOSS VAT return. — [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/eu/eu-e3-levy-low-value-e-commerce-import-package-july-2026/); [FedEx EU de minimis](https://www.fedex.com/en-ca/shipping-services/international/regulatory/eu-de-minimis-removal.html)
- Sources disagree on the counting unit: some say "per item", others "per HS line" or per HS code plus origin combination. — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/eu-ends-eur150-duty-exemption-charging-shein-and-temu-eur3-per-item/) vs [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-ca/shipping-services/international/regulatory/eu-de-minimis-removal.html)
- B2B consignments to VAT-registered recipients pay standard duty rates, not the €3 flat duty. — [Avalara](https://www.avalara.com/blog/en/europe/2025/11/eu-end-150-customs-duty-exemption-2026.html); [Ibec](https://www.ibec.ie/sfa/news-insights-and-events/insights/2026/03/09/reminder---150-eur-customs-duty-exemption-threshold-to-be-removed-as-of-1-july-2026)
- The IOSS number must reach the carrier in the electronic customs data. Otherwise VAT is charged again at import. — [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/eu/eu-e3-levy-low-value-e-commerce-import-package-july-2026/); [ShippyPro IOSS/OSS 2026](https://www.shippypro.com/blog/en/ioss-and-oss-explained-eu-vat-rules-for-ecommerce-sellers-in-2026)
- The Ibec note says the 2028 end date is "expected", and delays are anticipated. — [Ibec](https://www.ibec.ie/sfa/news-insights-and-events/insights/2026/03/09/reminder---150-eur-customs-duty-exemption-threshold-to-be-removed-as-of-1-july-2026)

**Freight forwarders China→DE** (indicative, Sep 2026)
- SINO Shipping, Sep 2026 update: air freight $4.34/kg for shipments of 1,000 kg and up (−8% vs Aug), 5–6 days to FRA/MUC. LCL to Hamburg/Bremen $45/CBM, 26–37 days. — [SINO Shipping China→Germany](https://www.sino-shipping.com/zh/freight-china-germany/)

### Inferences
- At 5–20 kg, per-kg air freight rates for 1,000 kg and up do not apply. Small shipments hit minimum charges. Express couriers (DHL, UPS, FedEx) or agent lines are the realistic options, with DAP plus self-clearance on your own EORI or the courier's brokerage.
- A sealed booster box at about 0.4–1 kg is dense in value, so air or express is likely better than LCL until you are shipping several CBM.
- The duty rate for trading cards (HS 9504 40, "playing cards") still needs checking. My unverified recollection is that the EU third-country duty for 9504 40 00 is 0%. Check in TARIC before costing. If it is 0%, a B2B import pays only 19% EUSt, which is deductible.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether specific agents (Superbuy, CSSBuy, Sugargoo) changed their EU lines after July 2026, which IOSS number they use, or whether they can issue documents in a business recipient's name.
- No published October 2026 rates were found.
- No TARIC check of the HS 9504 40 duty rate was done.

Agents typically accept PayPal or cards (not verified per agent in this run). For B2B suppliers, Alibaba Trade Assurance or bank transfer is the normal route. Data on Wise, PayPal and Trade Assurance was not collected in this run.

## Q5: Authenticity risks, lead times, loss and damage, disputes

### Takeaway
Counterfeit and resealed SC product is a real risk. Look for factory shrink-wrap vent holes, the anti-counterfeit labels on SC boxes, and the embossed "Pokémon" stamp on SC foil cards. The ink test does not work on SC cards. Prices well below market are a red flag. Lead times: agent DDP lines take about 10–20 days, air freight 5–6 days plus handling, LCL 26–37 days. Customs inspections can open sealed boxes. No quantified loss or damage rates were found.

### Cited Findings
- Authentic SC boxes carry anti-counterfeit labels. Fully fake boxes exist, with fake wrap, box, packs and cards. — [elitefourum: How to tell if a booster box is fake](https://elitefourum.com/t/how-to-tell-if-a-booster-box-is-fake/16035)
- SC foil cards carry an embossed "Pokémon" stamp in a bottom corner. A foil card without it is fake. The English-style ink test is not reliable for SC cards. — [elitefourum: ENG vs SC cards](https://www.elitefourum.com/t/eng-vs-simplified-ch-cards/50780)
- Genuine factory shrink-wrap has small vent holes from automated wrapping; heat-resealed wrap does not. Resealed boxes often have the valuable packs swapped. — [Samurai Sword Tokyo: spot fake JP cards](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/blogs/news/how-to-spot-fake-japanese-pokemon-cards)
- Shrink-wrap and reseal guides recommend comparing against official images and treating unusually low prices as a warning sign. — [PokéFolio: resealed packs](https://www.pokefolio.eu/en/blog/how-to-spot-resealed-pokemon-packs/); [PokéFolio: shrink-wrap guide](https://www.pokefolio.eu/en/blog/fake-vs-real-pokemon-booster-box-shrink-wrap-guide/); [Geeky Domain: fake sealed red flags](https://geekydomain.com/blogs/guides/fake-sealed-pokemon-product-red-flags-to-know)
