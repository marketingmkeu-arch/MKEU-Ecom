# EU/UK shops selling Asian-language (CN/JP/KR) Pokémon TCG: shop profiles and business models

Research date: 27 Oct 2026. **Method note:** WebFetch was blocked by the session network policy for every shop domain tried (pokipair.com, giga.de, gate-to-the-games.de, krystalkollectz.com). So **all data below comes from web-search snippets** (search-engine summaries of the pages). Prices are "as indexed" and may be cached or stale. The "observed" date is the search date (27 Oct 2026), not necessarily the date the page was last updated. Cardmarket and eBay were not accessed directly.

---

## Q1: Which DE/NL/UK/EU shops openly sell Simplified Chinese and Japanese Pokémon sealed products, and what do they charge vs. Chinese MSRP?

### Takeaway
Simplified Chinese (SC) sealed product is sold openly in the EU by:
- specialist importers: PokiPair (IE), tcgviert (DE), Blazingtail (FR), Quest Corner (FR), Gengar.cz (CZ), Gate to the Games (DE)
- mass retailers' marketplaces: Netto-online, MediaMarkt, Manor (CH)
- many eBay.de/UK sellers

Gem Pack displays sell for roughly €55–90 in the EU. At an assumed 10 RMB/pack × 18 packs = 180 RMB (≈ €22), that is a **≈2.5–4× markup on Chinese MSRP**. The Traditional Chinese (TC) 30th Celebration display (20 packs) sold for €98.99–119.99 at Netto in Oct 2026.

## Q2: Local EU stock or dropship from China/HK/Japan? Stated delivery times?

### Takeaway
There are two clear camps:
- **EU-stock shops** dispatch in 1–3 days: Cardcosmos, Gate to the Games, tcgviert, Netto, Chaos Cards UK.
- **Asia-shipped sellers** take about 5 days by express courier: PokiPair B2B, Samurai Sword Tokyo, TCGHobby. The buyer bears customs.

Many eBay "EU" listings price in USD or are UK/US sellers, so buyers can face customs exposure.

### Cited Findings
- Cardcosmos: same-day dispatch before 10:00, DHL 1–3 working days, €4.99 — [cardcosmos](https://cardcosmos.de/en/blogs/news/where-can-you-buy-pokemon-cards-cheaply-a-price-comparison-insider-tip-for-collectors-2025)
- PokiPair B2B: tracked DHL/FedEx **from Asia**, typically within 5 days, not sea freight; customs and import charges are borne by the buyer — [pokipair B2B](https://www.pokipair.com/b2b-program-pokemon-pokipair/)
- PokiPair B2C: worldwide UPS tracked — [pokipair](https://www.pokipair.com/category/shipping/)
- Samurai Sword Tokyo ships from Tokyo, tracked and insured; orders cannot be combined — [SST](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/?p=82383)
- KrystalKollectz ships from the US; international delivery times vary; no P.O. boxes — [Trustpilot](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/krystalkollectz.com)
- eBay listings mark stock as "Auf Lager" / "direkt lieferbar" to signal EU stock (e.g. 151C at €129.95 incl. MwSt.) — [eBay.de](https://www.ebay.de/itm/376508003089)
- Several eBay listings price in USD (e.g. US $121.99 ≈ €105), i.e. non-EU sellers — [search summary](https://www.ebay.de/itm/177318464842)

### Inferences
- "Auf Lager", "inkl. MwSt." and "UK Dispatch" in titles are used as trust and value signals. EU stock earns a premium over Asia-shipped offers.
- PokiPair's B2B design amounts to wholesale dropshipping: an Asian consolidator ships cases direct to EU resellers. Intra-EU invoicing is VAT-reverse-charged via an Irish entity.

### Gaps
- No explicit delivery-time statements were obtained for Gate to the Games, tcgviert or Blazingtail (pages not fetchable).

### Takeaway

### Inferences
- Pre-orders work as **interest-free working capital**: the customer pays in full weeks before the shop pays its Asian supplier. They also capture peak hype pricing. Prices often fall at release (the SST data shows this), so pre-order margins sit above release-day margins.

### Gaps

## Q5: Public evidence on sourcing (distributors, HK wholesalers, agents)?

### Takeaway
The best public evidence is **PokiPair's B2B program**. Approved EU VAT-registered businesses buy **full sealed cases only** of SC (and possibly JP) Pokémon, shipped by express from Asia, with intra-EU reverse-charge VAT. This means EU shops buy through Asia-based consolidators rather than official EU distribution. TCGHobby states that Gem Pack Vol.7 is sold via "authorized hobby shops and Pokémon Flagship channels in Mainland China". That is the upstream channel resellers buy from.

### Cited Findings
- PokiPair B2B:
  - requires a registered EU business with a valid VAT number; most applicants are rejected
  - minimum order of 1 sealed case, no single boxes
  - private B2B store; "you do not get a supplier list"
  - covers "Simplified Chinese & Japanese Pokemon sealed cases", though its llms.txt mentions only SC (a conflict)
  - Source: [pokipair B2B](https://www.pokipair.com/b2b-program-pokemon-pokipair/); [llms.txt](https://pokipair.com/llms.txt)
- Mainland China retail channel: authorized hobby shops and Pokémon Flagship stores — [TCGHobby](https://www.tcghobby.com/en-gb/blogs/news/pokemon-gem-pack-vol-7-release-date-highlights-exclusive-art-rares)
- Gem Packs are made specifically for the Simplified Chinese market — [CardTrader](https://www.cardtrader.com/en/games/pokemon/expansions/gem-pack-vol-1/categories/pokemon-booster/blueprints_search)
- Sweden's TCGStore.se offers Pokémon distribution and B2B, but its language scope is not visible in the snippet — [tcgstore.se](https://tcgstore.se/en/pages/distribution-b2b-pokemon-kort)

### Inferences
- Likely supply chain: Chinese authorized hobby shops and flagship allocations → Chinese/HK consolidators (some EU-registered, like PokiPair-IE) → EU web shops, marketplaces and eBay sellers. There is no official TPCi EU distribution for SC product, so this is grey import, which is legal for genuine goods first sold outside the EEA. Trademark exhaustion questions are not analysed here.
