Research date: 2026-10-27. Method note: web search snippets only. Direct page fetches failed because the session's network allowlist blocks onlinemarktplatz.de, monsterdealz.de and help.cardmarket.com. ## 1. Channel fee structures and their effect on margin

### Takeaway
Selling privately on eBay.de inside Germany still has no sales fee, but a reseller importing stock for profit is a commercial seller and pays a category commission (7–14% flat in many categories since 1 July 2026, plus €0.35/€0.45 per order). Cardmarket is the cheapest marketplace (about 5% commission, unconfirmed). Own-shop costs are about 2.1% + €0.30 (Shopify Payments Basic) or 2.49–2.99% + fixed fee (PayPal). Whatnot EU charges about 6.67% + VAT commission plus about 2.9% processing. Plan for about 8–17% of channel cost on marketplaces and about 3–5% plus a monthly subscription on your own shop.

### Cited Findings

**eBay.de – private sellers**
- Private sellers in Germany pay no listing fee and no sales commission on domestic sales. This started in March 2023 as a Germany-only change. eBay help text quoted: "Der Verkauf von Artikeln auf eBay.de ist innerhalb Deutschlands kostenlos." — [cio.de](https://www.cio.de/article/3700252/ebay-deutschland-streicht-gebuehren-fuer-private-verkaeufer.html); [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/e-commerce/ebay-privatverkauf-wird-gebuehrenfrei); [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/ebay-streicht-verkaufsgebuehren-fuer-private-endkunden-verkaufsprovision-gruende-1537530/)
- German buyers pay no buyer-protection fee. Contrast: on eBay UK, FR and IT private-seller fees were moved to a buyer-protection fee. — [size.ly calculator](https://www.size.ly/tools/ebay-fee-calculator-de); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/ebay-ends-private-seller-fees-on-its-french-and-italian-sites-and-moves-the-cost-to-buyers-through-a-new-buyer-protection-fee/)
- Exceptions reported for private sellers: 5% of the total when the delivery address is outside Germany, plus 3% for foreign-currency payments. Separately, a fee of €19.04 is reported when a buyer-protection case is decided against the seller (older article). — summarised via [techbook](https://www.techbook.de/shop-pay/shops-marktplaetze/ebay-gebuehren-privatkaeufe-abgeschafft); [gegen-hartz.de](https://www.gegen-hartz.de/news/erhebliche-verschlechterungen-bei-ebay-fuer-private-verkaeufer) **[UNVERIFIED against eBay primary page]**
- Legal caveat: private status does not fit systematic resale of imported goods. eBay's community discusses "offensichtlich gewerbliche" sellers who list as private. — [community.ebay.de](https://community.ebay.de/t5/Gewerbliche-Verk%C3%A4ufer-innen/Offensichtlich-gewerblicher-Verk%C3%A4ufer-verkauft-privat-und-keinen/m-p/4932610/highlight/true). The platform DAC7 reporting duty (Cardmarket reports to BZSt annually) makes volume selling visible to tax authorities — [Cardmarket TaxRequirements](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/TaxRequirements)

**eBay.de – commercial sellers (gewerblich)**
- From 1 July 2026 the tiered commission in about 43 categories was replaced by one flat rate per category, between 7% and 14% depending on category. Used and refurbished items in selected categories get a flat 5%. — [onlinemarktplatz.de](https://onlinemarktplatz.de/267961/neue-ebay-gebuehren-fuer-gewebliche-verkaeufer-ab-1-juli-2026/); [repicks.de](https://repicks.de/ratgeber/ebay-gebuehren-juli-2026); [Händlerbund](https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/themen/marktplaetze/ebay-aendert-gebuehren-teurer-guenstiger); [wortfilter](https://wortfilter.de/ebay-verkaufsprovision-juli-2026/)
- Fixed fee per order: €0.35 for orders up to €10. Since February 2026 it is €0.45 for orders over €10 (one source says only in some categories). — [repicks.de](https://repicks.de/ratgeber/ebay-gebuehren-2026); [amzplus](https://amzplus-consulting.de/blog/ebay-gebuehren-2026)
- A JTL-oriented table lists "Sammeln & Seltenes" as "kategorieabhängig" for new items and 5% for used items. — [vlarom.de](https://www.vlarom.de/ebay-gebuehren-2026-jtl-haendler/). **The exact 2026 rate for the Sammelkartenspiele/TCG sealed category was NOT found.** Older calculators list 14% for most standard categories and 6.5–12% (as of 07.03.2025). — [ebayfeescalculator.com](https://www.ebayfeescalculator.com/de/de-ebay-gebuhrenrechner/)
- eBay's managed payments mean the payment fee is included in the commission. No separate PayPal fee applies on eBay. (General knowledge of eBay's model since 2021 — **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**.)

**Cardmarket**
- Seller tiers are Private, Professional and Powerseller. Powersellers are selected by Cardmarket in an annual review (first review on 1 Feb 2026), and only top sellers qualify. Professional sellers must provide phone, photo ID and business registration, and have no selling restrictions. Private accounts can be restricted if volume suggests commercial activity. — [Cardmarket Professional Seller Guide](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/professional-seller-guide); [Cardmarket news – Powerseller requirements](https://news.cardmarket.com/en/Magic/changes-to-powerseller-requirements)
- Commission: third-party 2026 guides cite about 5% seller commission ("6–8% all-in"). A competitor (Misprint) says the effective take is about 11% once instant-credit and trustee fees are added. — [cardpulse.club 2026 guide](https://www.cardpulse.club/blog/cardmarket-complete-guide-2026); [pokemonpricetracker](https://www.pokemonpricetracker.com/blog/posts/selling-pokemon-cards-in-2026-best-platforms-pricing); [misprint.com](https://www.misprint.com/posts/one-piece-tcg-marketplace-fees-compared). **Official fee page could not be retrieved, so the 5% figure is [UNVERIFIED]. No separate professional or Powerseller rate was confirmed.**
- Cardmarket has a "Pre-Sale" feature with eligibility rules (relevant for pre-orders on new sets). — [Cardmarket PreSales](https://help.cardmarket.com/PreSales)
- Competitor CardNexus: European sellers pay a 5% seller fee. — [cardnexus.com](https://cardnexus.com/en/blog/cardnexus-marketplace-is-live)

**Kleinanzeigen "Sicher bezahlen"**
- The buyer pays the service fee and the seller pays nothing. Fee reported as €0.50 + 4.5% of the price (example: €2.75 on a €50 item). An older source says €0.35 + 4.5%. — [monsterdealz](https://www.monsterdealz.de/magazin/kleinanzeigen-sicher-bezahlen); [e-recht24](https://www.e-recht24.de/ecommerce/13270-ebay-kleinanzeigen-kaeuferschutz.html) (conflict on the fixed component)
- Since 22 July 2026, Klarna "Sofort bezahlen" is available inside Sicher bezahlen at no extra fee. — [onlinemarktplatz.de](https://onlinemarktplatz.de/269058/klarna-bei-kleinanzeigen/)
- Inference for margin: the seller keeps 100%, but the buyer's 4.5% surcharge lowers the price buyers will accept. Kleinanzeigen is also a known scam vector — [web.de](https://web.de/magazine/digital/5-tipps-betrug-kleinanzeigen-fiasko-42731136)

**Shopify (DE)**
- Shopify Payments online card rate: Basic 2.1% + €0.30, Grow 1.8% + €0.30, Advanced 1.6% + €0.30. No extra Shopify transaction fee with Shopify Payments. With an external gateway the surcharge is 2.0% (Basic) or 1.0% (Grow). — [qualimero](https://qualimero.com/blog/shopify-gebuehren); [qualimero transaction fees](https://qualimero.com/blog/shopify-transaktionsgebuehren)
- Monthly plan price conflicts: Basic €28/month (per Shopify page as of May 2025) vs "33 € bis 384 €/Monat". About 25% discount for annual prepayment. — [qualimero Preise](https://qualimero.com/blog/shopify-preise); official [shopify.com/de/preise](https://shopify.com/de/preise) (not retrievable)
- Note: PayPal used through Shopify carries PayPal's own fee (below), and Shopify's surcharge may apply too. **[UNVERIFIED for 2026]**

**PayPal (DE merchant fees, as of 15 July 2026)**
- Goods and services payments: 2.49% + fixed fee. Standard checkout: 2.99% + fixed fee. — [PayPal DE merchant fees PDF, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.paypalobjects.com/marketing/ua/pdf/DE/de/de-de-merchant-fees-15-jul-2026.pdf)
- The fixed fee is reported by calculators as €0.35 domestic, or €0.39 for checkout. Micropayment option: 4.99% + €0.09. Refunds keep the original fee. FX conversion costs 3–4%. — [finanz.de](https://www.finanz.de/konto/paypal-gebuehren/); [yagemi](https://www.yagemi.de/blog/e-commerce/paypal-gebuehren-haendler/) **(fixed fee not in PDF snippet: [UNVERIFIED])**

**Whatnot (live shopping)**
- Whatnot is officially available in Germany but not yet widespread. It has operational hubs in Germany, Poland, Ireland and the UK, and is hiring "Head of Launch, Germany" and German-speaking sales and category roles. — [whatnot.com careers](https://www.whatnot.com/careers/roles/deb76377-8200-4927-86b7-cddd769e18af); [antwort.net](https://www.antwort.net/a/technologie/welche-erfahrungen-machen-nutzer-mit-whatnot-in-deutschland.html); [valueaddedresource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/whatnot-us-ad-campaign-ireland-expansion/)
- EU fees: 6.67% commission plus VAT on the final price, plus about 2.9% processing. With a valid VAT ID stored, fees are charged without VAT. — [lengow](https://blog.lengow.com/what-is-whatnot-everything-you-need-to-know/); [eurofiscalis (Aug 2026)](https://www.eurofiscalis.com/de/whatnot-mehrwertsteuer/). US guides cite 8% commission + 2.9% + $0.30, giving an all-in of 13.5–17% including labels. — [mycomicscollection](https://mycomicscollection.com/de/blog/whatnot-verkaufsgebuehren-was-sie-wirklich-zahlen/); [underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/whatnot-seller-fees-2026)
- UK is Whatnot's largest European market and second-largest globally (sales +281% YoY, sellers +360%). European seller count grew more than 600% in a year. Whatnot claims about 60% of live-shopping share in NA and EU (company claims). — [valueaddedresource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/whatnot-us-ad-campaign-ireland-expansion/); [TNW, 10 Jun 2025](https://thenextweb.com/news/live-selling-europe-side-hustle)
- Pokémon is described as the single most-sold category on Whatnot (US marketing guide). — [underpriced](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/selling-pokemon-cards-on-whatnot-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Illustrative channel take on a €100 sealed box sale (fees only, before shipping, VAT and COGS). These are approximations combining the above **[estimates]**:
  - Cardmarket about €5 (up to €11 with extras)
  - eBay commercial about €7.45–14.45 (7–14% + €0.45)
  - Whatnot EU about €9.6 (6.67% + 2.9%)
  - Shopify Basic + Payments about €2.40 plus about €1/order prorated subscription, but you carry all customer-acquisition cost
  - PayPal direct about €2.84
  - Kleinanzeigen €0 to the seller
- VAT matters more than fees. As a regular (non-Kleinunternehmer) seller, 19% VAT on the sale price is due (or differential taxation does not apply to new imports). Import VAT and customs are covered by another research stream. Kleinunternehmer status (since 2025: €25k previous-year / €100k current-year limits) removes VAT charging — **[UNVERIFIED in this session; check with sourcing notes]**.
- A typical efficient channel mix: Cardmarket for singles and sealed liquidity, Shopify or your own shop for pre-orders and community buyers, Whatnot/TikTok for breaks. eBay commercial is the most expensive "mainstream" channel after July 2026 unless the TCG category got a low flat rate.

## 2. Demand and price trends – Simplified Chinese (SC) and Japanese Pokémon in EU, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
SC products have become a mainstream Western collector product, mostly through Gem Packs, 151C and 30th Celebration. Prices follow a launch-premium-then-normalise pattern. Newer Gem Pack volumes launch at lower street prices, while discontinued volumes (Vol. 2) roughly double. Pokémon printed about 10 billion cards in FY ending March 2026, says it is at "maximum capacity", and is reprinting hot sets. That supports long-run supply growth and dampens speculative upside on in-print items. No dated EU-specific price series was found.

### Cited Findings

**Simplified Chinese**
- SC 30th Celebration booster box (20 packs × 6 cards, each pack with one of 30 special Pikachu): release listed as 16 Sep 2026. US retail asks are $89.50–$109.99, and several are sold out. An NL-based EU shop asks €129.95. eBay.com listings range $75–114, with China-based sellers at about $77–90 plus import. — [dotscardshop](https://dotscardshop.com/products/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-booster-box-simplified-chinese); [collectorstore](https://collectorstore.com/products/pokemon-chinese-30th-celebration-booster-box); [card-binder.com (NL, €129.95)](https://card-binder.com/products/pokemon-tcg-simplified-chinese-30th-celebration-booster-box); [eBay search](https://www.ebay.com/shop/chinese-30th-anniversary-pokemon?_nkw=chinese+30th+anniversary+pokemon)
  - Inference: about $80 at source vs about €130 EU retail ask, a gross spread of roughly 40–60% before shipping, import VAT and duty, and fees. That spread is typical early after release and tends to compress.
- Gem Packs: Volume 6 launched "well below the Chinese MSRP that Volume 5 held". Once a volume stops being restocked, prices climb. Vol. 2 (Eeveelution) boxes now cost more than double the newest volumes. — [tcgtalk Vol. 6 guide (2026)](https://tcgtalk.com/guides/gem-volume-6-chinese-pull-rates); [playvault.ae](https://playvault.ae/blogs/guides/pokemon-gem-packs-chinese-booster-boxes-explained)
- Graded single example: SC Gem Pack Vol. 2 Umbreon full art CGC 10 sold for $250 (1 Sep 2026) vs a 23-sale average of $336.52 and a high of $475. CardLadder growth rate is −41%, showing post-hype decline for chase singles. — [CardLadder](https://www.cardladder.com/ladder/card/yPGc0chMLwhAEYvOf8YO)
- Street pricing range: Vol. 1 box about $79 (TCG Hobby), Vol. 2 box $79.95 (KrystalKollectz). SimplifiedTCG lists per-pack prices from $3.99 (Vol. 6) to $11.99 (Vol. 1). Listings are undated. — [simplifiedtcg](https://simplifiedtcg.com/collections/gem-packs/); [tcghobby](https://www.tcghobby.com/products/simplified-chinese-pokemon-gem-pack); [krystalkollectz](https://krystalkollectz.com/blogs/news/gem-pack-volume-2-pokemon-simplified-chinese-card-list-krystalkollectz)
- Gem Pack Badge collection prices are tracked on PriceCharting (a dedicated SC section exists, a sign of Western demand). — [PriceCharting](https://www.pricecharting.com/game/pokemon-chinese-gem-pack/gem-pack-badge-collection)
- German eBay has many SC listings (Gem Pack Vol. 3 "CBB3C", 151C "Hoffnung" slim box, SWSH SC boxes), so competition on eBay.de exists. — [ebay.de 157395131930](https://www.ebay.de/itm/157395131930); [ebay.de 357463174635](https://www.ebay.de/itm/357463174635)
- 151C became highly popular because of the China-exclusive Pikachu AR, followed by a surge in resealed boxes. — [LegitApp](https://legitapp.com/it/blog/real-vs-fake-how-to-spot-a-fake-pokemon-tcg-151-journey-slim-booster-box-chinese-exclusive-pikachu-ar)

**Japanese (PriceCharting sold data, USD, mostly eBay)**
- Terastal Festival (sv8a) box: mid–late Oct 2026 sales about $72–95 typical (outliers $139 and $180). — [PriceCharting](https://www.pricecharting.com/game/pokemon-japanese-terastal-festival/booster-box)
- Battle Partners (sv9): Aug–Sep 2026 about $70–87. — [PriceCharting](https://www.pricecharting.com/game/pokemon-japanese-battle-partners/booster-box)

- 25th Anniversary Collection (s8a): late Sep–early Oct 2026 about $235–415. — [PriceCharting](https://www.pricecharting.com/game/pokemon-japanese-25th-anniversary-collection/booster-box)
- Japanese retail pack price increase in May 2026, so M1–M4 were the last boxes at the ¥5,400 retail tier. Mega Brave sealed boxes cost ¥9,000–10,500 (May 2026). — [samuraiswordtokyo](https://www.samuraiswordtokyo.com/blogs/news/best-japanese-pokemon-booster-box-2026)
  - Inference: higher JP retail prices squeeze import margins on in-print JP boxes. Terastal Festival at about $72–95 after release suggests in-print JP boxes trade near import cost.

**Supply and reprint behaviour**
- About 10 billion Pokémon cards were printed in FY ending March 2026, roughly flat YoY, with a lifetime total over 85 billion. — [pokeguardian](https://www.pokeguardian.com/3191786_pokemon-cards-continue-strong-sales-as-total-production-hits-85-billion); [ingamenews (May 2026)](https://www.ingamenews.com/2026/05/pokemon-trading-card-game-sees-10.html)

- Pokémon Center launched a Germany storefront, its first for mainland Europe. This is more official direct-to-consumer supply in DE and competes with resellers on English and German product. — [bulbagarden](https://bulbagarden.net/threads/pokemon-center-online-soft-launches-new-storefront-for-germany.308671/)

**Other TCGs (demand signals)**
- One Piece: TCGplayer "cards climbing in price" (19 Aug 2026). TCGtalk (Apr 2026): raw cards rising while graded slabs are selling off. — [TCGplayer](https://seller.tcgplayer.com/blog/price-trends-one-piece-cards-climbing-in-price-08-19-2026); [tcgtalk](https://tcgtalk.com/blog/tcg-market-weekly-april-30-2026)
- Kayou (Naruto etc.): official European debut at Spielwarenmesse 2026, Nuremberg, focused on DE, FR, UK, IT and ES through local distributors. These are collectibles with no game format. Bandai's Naruto Mythos TCG has been sold in Europe since 13 Mar 2026, and a Bandai Naruto TCG global launch is planned for 2027. German eBay asks are low (e.g. 900+ card bulk for €12). — [bolsamania press release](https://www.bolsamania.com/nota-de-prensa/mercados/kayou-expands-pan-entertainment-ecosystem-in-europe-partnership-and-localized-strategy--21628558.html); [gamertagmythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/naruto-tcg/naruto-kayou-cards-explained); [narutotcgmythos](https://www.narutotcgmythos.com/naruto-mythos-tcg-the-first-expansion-now-has-an-official-release-date); [respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/bandai-revives-naruto-trading-card-game-for-global-2027-launch)
  - Inference: Kayou's official EU distribution erodes the grey-import margin on Kayou products in DE.

### Inferences
- Pattern: SC and JP sealed sells at a premium in launch weeks (sold-out signals, €130 asks for a ~$80 source box). It then converges toward landed cost plus a thin margin while in print. Value comes back only after the product goes out of print (Gem Pack Vol. 2 doubling). For a small reseller, money is made fast at launch or by holding out-of-print stock, not in between.
- Chase singles from SC sets can decline sharply (−41% on a Gem Pack Umbreon CGC 10), so "open and sell singles" economics are volatile.

### Gaps
- No dated EU (Cardmarket) price series for SC 30th Celebration, Gem Packs, 151C or SC Terastal. Cardmarket was unreachable, so the trend claims rely on USD sources.
- No hard data on Chinese-market production volume increases specifically (TPC China / SC print runs). Only global TPC totals were found.
- No source on SC Terastal-equivalent set pricing in EU.
- Lorcana 2025–2026 demand in DE was not researched (budget).

## 3. Proven growth tactics of small TCG resellers

### Takeaway
Evidence points to four main levers: pre-orders on capped allocations (Riftbound and Cyberpunk launches show sell-outs), live selling and breaks (Whatnot growing fast in Europe; Pokémon is its top category), presence at conventions (SPIEL Essen), and diversification into newly launched TCGs. Systematic case studies of small German resellers were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Riftbound (Riot / UVS): EN release on 31 Oct 2025 in Europe with capped preorders that sold out. Riot published "Origins Launch Learnings" acknowledging that customers were still waiting on preorders. Riot confirmed a reprint. In Jan 2026 stores were selling boxes at "exorbitant prices" with scalping, and availability was expected to stay tight until H2 2026. The product was totally out of stock in France. — [playriftbound.com](https://playriftbound.com/en-us/news/announcements/riftbound-origins-launch-learnings/); [cardsrealm Jan 2026](https://riftbound.cardsrealm.com/en-gb/articles/riftbound-the-january-announcement-and-the-signs-for-2026); [sheepesports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/riftbound-global-release-set-for-october-31-exclusive-arcane-box-revealed-pre-orders-opening-on/en)
- Riftbound cadence (German retailer cardcosmos.de): Spiritforged 13 Feb 2026, Unleashed 8 May 2026, Vendetta 31 Jul 2026, Radiance Q4 2026. Several are listed as sold out or on preorder. A French-language version is due mid-2026. A China-exclusive Miss Fortune promo sold for $9,000 in China. — [cardcosmos.de](https://cardcosmos.de/en/collections/riftbound-release-2026); [sheepesports FR](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/riftbound-will-become-available-in-french-in-mid-2026/en); [sheepesports promo](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/riftbound-prestige-miss-fortune-promo-card-sells-for-usd9-000-in-china/en)

At SPIEL Essen 2026 (22–25 Oct) WeirdCo had booth 5G700 (Hall 5) with learn-to-play demos, retail space selling sealed product, and a promo stop. **No data was found on actual sales or demand at SPIEL.** — [ICv2](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/63620/cyberpunk-tcg-retail-edition-incoming); [ppe.pl](https://www.ppe.pl/news/424444/nowy-cyberpunk-z-data-premiery-tworcy-przygotowali-caly-harmonogram.html); [gamertagmythras convention schedule](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/cyberpunk-tcg/cyberpunk-tcg-convention-schedule-promos); [CD Projekt](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/media/news/cyberpunk-trading-card-game-launches-kickstarter-campaign/); [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/cyberpunk-tcg-makes-tabletop-history-with-27m-campaign)

- Live selling: Whatnot's European seller count grew more than 600% in a year, and UK sales grew 281% YoY. Pokémon is the most-sold category (US). Pack breaks are a core Whatnot format. — [valueaddedresource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/whatnot-us-ad-campaign-ireland-expansion/); [underpriced Whatnot Pokémon guide](https://www.underpriced.app/blog/selling-pokemon-cards-on-whatnot-guide-2026); [TNW 2025](https://thenextweb.com/news/live-selling-europe-side-hustle)

### Inferences
- Product-launch arbitrage (Riftbound, Cyberpunk TCG, SC 30th Celebration) is the strongest documented margin window. It requires secured allocation before release, and pre-orders financed by customers reduce capital risk.
- Whatnot DE is early-stage (hiring launch staff), so first-mover sellers may get platform support and less competition. Breaks also let you monetise low-value SC/JP packs (Gem Packs at $4–12/pack) as entertainment rather than as unit sales. ## 4. Main business risks

### Takeaway
The biggest risks are (a) price normalisation and overstock after launch as Pokémon prints at record volumes and reprints hot sets, (b) counterfeits and reseals concentrated in China-sourced SC/JP sealed product, (c) platform and legal risk of trading as a "private" seller, (d) competition from official EU channels (Pokémon Center DE, Kayou EU distribution) and established import shops, and (e) payment and chargeback exposure.

### Cited Findings
- **Price drops:** Gem Pack Vol. 6 launched below Vol. 5's MSRP. A Gem Pack Vol. 2 chase single shows −41% growth on CardLadder. Global supply is about 10 billion cards per year with active reprints. — [tcgtalk](https://tcgtalk.com/guides/gem-volume-6-chinese-pull-rates); [CardLadder](https://www.cardladder.com/ladder/card/yPGc0chMLwhAEYvOf8YO); [comicbook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/new-report-confirms-pokemon-tcg-massively-increased-production-in-2026-but-its-still-not-enough/)
- Riftbound reprint confirmation shows the hype premium on new TCGs is temporary by publisher design. — [cardsrealm](https://riftbound.cardsrealm.com/en-gb/articles/riftbound-the-january-announcement-and-the-signs-for-2026)
- Graded One Piece slabs were "selling off" in April 2026 while raw cards rose (risk for grading-based strategies). — [tcgtalk](https://tcgtalk.com/blog/tcg-market-weekly-april-30-2026)
- **Counterfeits and reseals:** Surge in resealed SC 151C boxes. Reported tells: wrong shrink, poor print, non-working QR code, missing barcode, haphazard pack arrangement. — [LegitApp](https://legitapp.com/it/blog/real-vs-fake-how-to-spot-a-fake-pokemon-tcg-151-journey-slim-booster-box-chinese-exclusive-pikachu-ar); [SNKRDUNK](https://snkrdunk.com/en/magazine/?p=412254); [pokefolio.eu shrink-wrap guide](https://www.pokefolio.eu/en/blog/fake-vs-real-pokemon-booster-box-shrink-wrap-guide/)

- Trustpilot and eBay complaints describe boxes arriving with fake cards or damaged seals (e.g. Pokeboxshop reviews). — [trustpilot](https://pl.trustpilot.com/review/pokeboxshop.com)
- **Platform and tax risk:** Cardmarket restricts private accounts with commercial-level volume and reports seller data to the BZSt annually. eBay community flags "obviously commercial" sellers listed as private. — [Cardmarket guide](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/professional-seller-guide); [Cardmarket Tax](https://help.cardmarket.com/en/TaxRequirements); [community.ebay.de](https://community.ebay.de/t5/Gewerbliche-Verk%C3%A4ufer-innen/Offensichtlich-gewerblicher-Verk%C3%A4ufer-verkauft-privat-und-keinen/m-p/4932610/highlight/true)

Kayou has official EU distribution. eBay.de carries many China-based SC listings at about $77–90. — [card-binder](https://card-binder.com/products/pokemon-tcg-simplified-chinese-30th-celebration-booster-box); [bulbagarden](https://bulbagarden.net/threads/pokemon-center-online-soft-launches-new-storefront-for-germany.308671/); [bolsamania](https://www.bolsamania.com/nota-de-prensa/mercados/kayou-expands-pan-entertainment-ecosystem-in-europe-partnership-and-localized-strategy--21628558.html); [eBay](https://www.ebay.com/shop/chinese-30th-anniversary-pokemon?_nkw=chinese+30th+anniversary+pokemon)
- **Fee creep:** eBay raised the fixed fee to €0.45 (Feb 2026) and moved to flat category rates (July 2026). PayPal raised its base rate over time (t3n: 1.9% to 2.49%). — [repicks](https://repicks.de/ratgeber/ebay-gebuehren-2026); [t3n](https://t3n.de/news/?p=1097939)

### Inferences
- China-based eBay sellers shipping SC boxes directly to EU buyers set a price ceiling. A DE reseller must win on trust (authenticity guarantee, fast domestic shipping, German-language service), not on price.
- Authentication capability (QR checks, shrink comparison, buying only from verified distributors) is a core competency and a marketing differentiator.
- Hold inventory short for in-print products. Hold long only for clearly out-of-print SKUs.

- No list or market-share data on major German/EU Asian-TCG import shops (e.g. their pricing for SC sets).
- Legal status of paid box breaks (Glücksspiel concerns) in Germany was not researched.
